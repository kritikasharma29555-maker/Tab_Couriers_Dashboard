# 📊 TAB Couriers Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to evaluate core business metrics, operational efficiency, fuel consumption, and regional risk factors for **TAB Couriers**.

##  Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling, interactive visual canvas design, and report layout.
* **DAX** – Measures for tracking total revenue, fuel usage, tonnage, and delivery zone risk ratios.
* **Power Query (M)** – Data transformation, cleaning, and preparation.
* **Logistics Data Analysis** – Operational KPI tracking, zone surcharge risk assessment, and trend analysis.



##  Dashboard Structure & Key Features

### 1. Executive Performance Overview
* **Core Business KPIs:** Top-level metrics tracking overall revenue, tonnage, ship volume, and fuel consumption.
* **Geographic Volume Distribution:** Highlighting top destination hubs led by **Gurgaon (23.16%)**, **Bangalore (20.14%)**, and **Hyderabad (31.1%)**.
* **Revenue Per KG:** Evaluating yield efficiency across destinations (e.g., Karnal, Hissar, Saharanpur averaging ₹20/KG).
* **Route Analysis:** Origin-to-destination transaction tracking (e.g., Chennai to Gurgaon generating ₹8.74L).

### 2. Logistics & Operational Efficiency
* **Fuel Consumption Analysis:** Tracking high-usage hubs—Gurgaon (107K L), Hyderabad (98K L), and Khoda (97K L).
* **Delivery Zone Surcharge Risk:** Segmenting deliveries by delivery zones (**NO ODA**, **ODA**, and **ROI**) to identify surcharge risk zones.
* **Monthly Revenue Trends:** Line trend showcasing revenue fluctuations from peak periods (~₹1.12M) down to a low of ~₹692K, followed by a recovery towards ~₹900K.



## 💡 Key Operational Insights

* **Top Revenue & Fuel Drivers:** Gurgaon (₹953K, 107K L fuel), Hyderabad (₹864K, 98K L fuel), and Khoda (₹882K, 97K L fuel) drive the highest volume and fuel expense.
* **Surcharge Risk Zones:** High concentration of Out-of-Delivery-Area (ODA) shipments in **Khoda (98.8% ODA)** and **Jhajjar (93.8% ODA)** indicates areas requiring delivery network optimization or freight rate reviews.
* **Seasonal Revenue Dynamics:** Monthly revenue experienced an early-year peak of ₹1.12M before contracting to a low of ₹692K in July/August, followed by a steady recovery back to the ~₹900K threshold by year-end.
